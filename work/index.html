/ Why ongoing support

A site is not finished when it launches.

Those numbers are not what the sites scored on launch day. They are what they score now, because someone has been watching them since.

01

Browsers ship every few weeks

Chrome and Safari update constantly. Something that renders correctly today (a layout, a form, a checkout step) can break quietly months later, and nobody files a bug. Someone has to keep looking.

02

Google keeps moving the line

The thresholds behind these scores get revised. INP replaced FID as a Core Web Vital in 2024. A perfect score is a reading from one day, not a permanent state.

03

Dependencies age into vulnerabilities

Every framework, plugin and library accumulates published security advisories. Left unpatched, a site becomes a public list of known ways in.

04

Content is what slows a site down

Launch figures are measured on an empty site. Real ones fill up with unoptimised images, embeds and marketing tags, and that is where the seconds come from.
