About the founder

Ibrahim Hussein founded kaprera in Beirut to give growing businesses software that fits the way they already work. He leads every project himself, from the first discovery call to launch, so clients explain their business once, to the person who designs and builds their system.

He works in Arabic and English, and has delivered systems and websites for clients in Lebanon and Saudi Arabia: a pest control company, a flower shop, a youth-led NGO and an investment firm among them.
