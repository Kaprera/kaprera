About kaprera.

kaprera is a software agency in Beirut, Lebanon. We build custom business systems that replace Excel sheets, WhatsApp orders and paperwork with one system built around how your team already works. We also build MVPs for startups, bilingual websites and landing pages.

/ Our vision

Every growing business in the Middle East runs on one system built around how its team works, not on manual work.

/ Our mission

We replace Excel sheets, WhatsApp orders and paperwork with custom business systems built around how your team already works.

/ Our pillars

How we work with every client.

  1. 01

    Built around you

    We start from how your team already works, then shape the system to fit it. Not a ready-made tool you have to adapt to.

  2. 02

    Fast to value

    A first working version with your real data by day 7, and the full system live in 3 weeks. An MVP for a startup takes 1 week.

  3. 03

    Plain and honest

    A fixed quote before any work starts, plain words without jargon, and we tell you when a ready-made tool is the better fit.
